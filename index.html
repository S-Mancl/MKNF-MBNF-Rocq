Propositional MKNF and MBNF, mechanized

An introduction to reasoning with incomplete information, with definitions and proofs in Rocq. The chapters build from formulas and their meaning to reasoning algorithms and a worked investigation.

Download the Rocq sources ZIP Read the report PDF

Read or work on the sources locally as you follow the chapters. The archive includes a Makefile for building them.

1. Syntax

Write formulas about facts, knowledge, belief, and negation as failure.

2. Semantics

Learn how worlds give formulas a meaning, which structures count as models, and when a conclusion follows.

3. Algorithms and correctness

Turn the model definitions into procedures and prove that their computed answers agree with the semantics.

4. The case of Mr. Charlie

Follow the deductions as new clues change the investigation.
